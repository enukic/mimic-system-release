## Mimic System Releases

Welcome to the official release repository for the Mimic device firmware and the Supply Drop update utility.

## 📥 Download the Updater

To update your Mimic device to the latest firmware, download the Supply Drop utility for Windows:

* Download Supply Drop

## 🔄 How to Update Your Mimic

1. Download and launch the Supply Drop application.

2. Ensure your computer is connected to the internet. The app will automatically check for and download the latest firmware in the background.

3. Connect your Mimic device.

4. Once the app says Ready, click Start Update.

5. The device will automatically reboot into Bootloader mode and flash the new firmware.

## 🛠 Troubleshooting

* Device not detected: Ensure your USB cable supports data transfer.

* Offline Mode: If the app says "Offline mode", check your internet connection and restart the app to fetch the latest firmware.

* Update Failed: Close other applications that might be communicating with COM ports or Xbox controllers and try again.

*Note*: This repository hosts the metadata used by the Supply Drop app to deliver seamless, over-the-air updates.
